# ISO/IEC and CIS mapping notes

Use ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 to connect technical AI safeguards to organizational security and AI management processes. Use CIS Controls as a source of foundational enterprise security practices. The governance, identity, data, supply-chain, infrastructure, monitoring, and incident-response references provide starting points.

This package does not map controls clause by clause and does not establish compliance. Verify the licensed/current standard or current CIS publication, identify the applicable scope, and document evidence and exceptions before making an audit or conformity claim.
